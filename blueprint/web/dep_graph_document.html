Theorem 1.4Homothetic invariance, normalization, and finite orientation-cover reduction

Under homothetic metric rescaling \((\mathsf{d}, \mathcal{H}^N) \mapsto (\lambda \mathsf{d}, \lambda ^N \mathcal{H}^N)\) (\(\lambda {\gt} 0\)), volume entropy scales by \(\lambda ^{-1}\) and Hausdorff volume by \(\lambda ^N\), leaving \(h(\overline{X})^N \mathcal{H}^N(X)\) invariant.
