A non-collapsed \(\mathsf{RCD}(K, N)\) space \((X, \mathsf{d}, \mathcal{H}^N)\) carries the regular set \(\mathcal{R}_N(X)\) (\(\theta _N(x) = 1\)), the open topological manifold locus \(\mathcal{M}(X) \supseteq \mathcal{R}_N(X)\), the closed singular set \(\mathcal{S}(X) = X \setminus \mathcal{M}(X)\), and singular strata \(\mathcal{S}^k(X)\). Closed Alexandrov \(N\)-spaces of curvature \(\ge \kappa \) without boundary embed into closed non-collapsed \(\mathsf{RCD}((N-1)\kappa , N)\) spaces via AlexandrovSpaceWithoutBoundary.

On any closed non-collapsed \(\mathsf{RCD}(K, N)\) space (and in particular on any closed Alexandrov \(N\)-space without boundary), \(\mathcal{R}_N(X)\) and \(\mathcal{M}(X)\) have full \(\mathcal{H}^N\)-measure, \(\mathcal{S}(X) \subseteq X \setminus \mathcal{R}_N(X) \subseteq \mathcal{S}^{N-2}(X)\) is closed with \(\mathcal{H}^{N-1}(\mathcal{S}(X)) = \mathcal{H}^{N-1}(X \setminus \mathcal{R}_N(X)) = 0\), \(\mathcal{H}^N(X) {\gt} 0\), and every point \(x \notin \mathcal{R}_N(X)\) satisfies the strict Bishop–Gromov density gap \(\theta _N(x) {\lt} 1\).

Proof ▶

Outer-measure monotonicity along \(\mathcal{S}(X) \subseteq X \setminus \mathcal{R}_N(X) \subseteq \mathcal{S}^{N-2}(X)\) and De Philippis–Gigli characterization \(\theta _N(x) = 1 \iff x \in \mathcal{R}_N(X)\) with \(\theta _N(x) \le 1\).
