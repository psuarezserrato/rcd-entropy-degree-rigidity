For a probability weight \(w\) supported at points \(y_i\) in a metric space \((Y, \mathsf{d}_Y)\) and reference basepoint \(o \in Y\), Sturm’s renormalized \(d^2\)-barycenter functional is \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(z) = \sum _i w_i (\mathsf{d}_Y(y_i, z)^2 - \mathsf{d}_Y(o, y_i)^2)\). Together with the \(\mathrm{CAT}(0)\) semi-parallelogram midpoint predicate IsCATZeroMidpoint, this supports the lifted geodesic homotopy \(\overline{H}_s(x, t)\) and its quotient descent \(H_s : X \times [0, 1] \to Y\).

Sturm’s summand satisfies \(|\mathsf{d}(y, z)^2 - \mathsf{d}(o, y)^2| \le \mathsf{d}(o, z)(\mathsf{d}(o, z) + 2\, \mathsf{d}(o, y))\), requiring only finite first moments, and shifting \(o_1 \mapsto o_2\) adds a constant independent of \(z\). Consequently, \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}\) is continuous and shares exact global minimizers with metricEnergy.

Proof ▶

Factor \(a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)\) with triangle inequality bounds and note that \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(z) = 2\, \mathrm{metricEnergy}(w, y, z) - \sum _i w_i\, \mathsf{d}(o, y_i)^2\).

Under IsCATZeroMidpoint, \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}\) satisfies the strong midpoint convexity deficit \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(m) \le \frac{1}{2}\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(a) + \frac{1}{2}\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(b) - \frac{1}{4}\mathsf{d}_Y(a, b)^2\). Any minimizer \(q = \mathrm{bar}(\nu )\) therefore satisfies Sturm’s variance inequality \(\frac{1}{2}\mathsf{d}_Y(q, z)^2 \le \mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(z) - \mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}(q)\) and is the unique global minimizer.

Proof ▶

Weighted summation of the \(\mathrm{CAT}(0)\) semi-parallelogram law and comparison at \(m = \mathrm{mid}(q, z)\) against minimality at \(q\).

For any isometric \(\pi _1(X)\)-representation \(\rho : \Gamma \curvearrowright \widetilde{Y}\), unique minimization forces \(\mathrm{bar}(\rho (\gamma )_* \nu ) = \rho (\gamma )\, \mathrm{bar}(\nu )\). Combining with \(\Gamma \)-equivariance of \(w_s(x, z)\) yields \(\rho \)-equivariance of the lifted natural map \(\overline{F}_s\) and of the geodesic homotopy \(\overline{H}_s\), which therefore descends to a homotopy \(H_s : X \times [0, 1] \to Y\) between \(f\) (\(t = 0\)) and \(F_s\) (\(t = 1\)).

Proof ▶

Isometry covariance of \(\mathcal{S}_{w, y, o}\) transfers the minimizing property of \(q\) to \(\rho (\gamma ) q\), and uniqueness yields equality.
