For any continuous map \(f : X \to Y\) (\(X\) compact, \(Y\) Hausdorff), \(U_f \subseteq \mathcal{M}(X)\), \(\Sigma _f\) is compact and closed, and \(V_f, U_f\) are open. When \(f\) is Lipschitz, \(\mathcal{H}^{N-1}(\Sigma _f) = 0\) and \(\mathcal{H}^N(V_f) = \mathcal{H}^N(Y)\).

Proof ▶

Continuous image of the closed compact set \(\mathcal{S}(X)\) and Lipschitz Hausdorff bound \(\mathcal{H}^{N-1}(f(\mathcal{S}(X))) \le \mathrm{Lip}(f)^{N-1}\mathcal{H}^{N-1}(\mathcal{S}(X)) = 0\).

A Lipschitz map \(f : X \to Y\) satisfies \(\mathbf{M}(f_\# T) = |\deg (f)|\, \mathcal{H}^N(Y)\), \(\deg (f) = \mathcal{H}^N(Y)^{-1} \int _Y \deg (f; U_X, y)\, d\mathcal{H}^N(y)\), and \(|\deg (f)|\, \mathcal{H}^N(Y) \le \mathrm{Lip}(f)^N \mathcal{H}^N(X)\) (in particular \(\mathcal{H}^N(Y) \le \mathcal{H}^N(X)\) when \(f\) is \(1\)-Lipschitz and \(\deg (f) \ne 0\)).

Proof ▶

Combines \(f_\# T = \deg (f)[\! [Y ]\! ]\) with mass homogeneity \(\mathbf{M}(f_\# T) \le \mathrm{Lip}(f)^N \mathbf{M}(T)\).

Since \(\partial T = 0\) and \(\mathcal{H}^{N+1}(Y) = 0\), Wenger’s homotopy formula forces \((f_1)_\# T = (f_0)_\# T\) and \(\deg (f_1) = \deg (f_0)\) across Lipschitz homotopies. Any map with \(\deg (f) \ne 0\) is surjective, degree multiplies across covers (\(\deg (p \circ \hat{f}) = \deg (p)\deg (\hat{f})\)), and Mitsuishi’s homological index satisfies \(\mathrm{ind}_H(f) \mid |\deg (f)|\) and \(\mathrm{ind}_H(f) \le |\deg (f)|\).

Proof ▶

Injectivity of \(d \mapsto d[\! [Y ]\! ]\) equates degrees from \((f_1)_\# T = (f_0)_\# T\); if \(\mathrm{range}(f)\) missed an open ball \(U \subseteq Y\), positive measure of \(U\) would contradict support nullity of \(\deg (f)[\! [Y ]\! ]\).
