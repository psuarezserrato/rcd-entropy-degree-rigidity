Definition 1.1 Logarithmic ball-volume rate, exponential growth, and entropy-volume product

For a metric-measure space \((Z, \mathsf{d}, \mathfrak {m})\) and basepoint \(x_0 \in Z\), the logarithmic ball-volume rate is \(R^{-1} \log \mathfrak {m}(B(x_0, R))\), exponential volume growth \(\mathfrak {m}(B(x_0, R)) \le C e^{h R}\) is captured by HasExponentialVolumeGrowth, the normalized exponential weights \(w_s(x, z)\) normalize \(e^{-s\, \mathsf{d}(x, z)}\) over finite samples, and the scale-invariant entropy-volume product is \(h(\overline{X})^N \mathcal{H}^N(X)\).

By the triangle inclusion \(B(x_0, R) \subseteq B(x_1, R + \mathsf{d}(x_0, x_1))\), exponential volume growth with exponent \(h\) transfers across all basepoints \(x_0, x_1 \in Z\) and bounds \(R^{-1}\log \mathfrak {m}(B(x_0, R)) \le h + R^{-1}\log C\). Moreover, the hyperbolic model density satisfies \((\sinh (\kappa r)/\kappa )^{N-1} \le (2\kappa )^{-(N-1)} e^{(N-1)\kappa r}\), giving the Bishop–Gromov upper bound \(h(\overline{X}) \le (N-1)\kappa \) under \(\mathsf{RCD}(-(N-1)\kappa ^2, N)\).

Proof ▶

Triangle inequality ball monotonicity and the elementary estimate \(2\sinh (u) \le e^u\) raised to the \((N-1)\)-st power.

Under homothetic metric rescaling \((\mathsf{d}, \mathcal{H}^N) \mapsto (\lambda \mathsf{d}, \lambda ^N \mathcal{H}^N)\) (\(\lambda {\gt} 0\)), volume entropy scales by \(\lambda ^{-1}\) and Hausdorff volume by \(\lambda ^N\), leaving \(h(\overline{X})^N \mathcal{H}^N(X)\) invariant. Passing to the \(k\)-sheeted intermediate cover \(\widehat{Y} \to Y\) associated to \(f_* \pi _1(X) \le \pi _1(Y)\) with \(|\deg (f)| = k |\deg (\hat{f})|\) and normalizing by \(c = h(\overline{X})/h(\widetilde{Y})\) yields \(|\deg (\hat{f})| = 1\), \(c^{-1} h(\overline{X}) = h(\widetilde{Y})\), and \(c^N \mathcal{H}^N(X) = \mathcal{H}^N(\widehat{Y})\).

Proof ▶

Cancellation of \(\lambda ^{-N}\lambda ^N = 1\) and positive factor \(k {\gt} 0\) in the covering degree factorization.
